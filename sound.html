My work in minimal house and techno music.

music

Electronic music is obviously the music of the modern subject. My musical work concentrates in minimal house and techno, and this is an area that's uniquely able to synthesize free expression and organic rhythm with repetition, causality, process, and modular, industrialized thinking.

I use percussion to speak musically because it is broad and non-linear, more encompassing than any other musical layer. It's tied most intimately to the human physical language of dance and movement.

I play regularly in Austin, with the intention of exposing the scene to sounds that reflect how we hear the world in 2026 and beyond.

preoccupations

Rhythm in industrialized processes

The mania of repetition, as a unifying factor between human trance states and industrial society.

Causality and process in organic rhythm

We use artistic words — call and response, rhythm, groove — but these concepts can be dressed up in modern terminology: causal chains, systems, temporal structures. Blending mechanized music with organic music roots out the sonic spaces linking the two, or indeed blurs the line between them.

Modular thinking and minimalism as a linking factor

Minimal techno and house are the most excellent representation of this, because of the artistic choice to constrain a track to a minimal and basic set of sonic elements. It is the same impulse as industrial society's, and it was of course guided by the development of mechanized sonic tools — the modular synthesizer among them.

Curation and consensus
