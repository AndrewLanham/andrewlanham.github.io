case study — the redesign

Converting a keg-based commercial coffee system line to post-mix, unlocking a market five times larger.

In early 2025, I came on as engineering lead for a small partnership redesigning commercial beverage dispensing hardware. The existing system was keg-based — nitrogen tanks, pressure-fed tubing — which only worked in buildings of 150+ units: office towers and large apartment complexes.

Working directly between the people who understood the deployment problem and the manufacturing constraints, I led the redesign to a post-mix architecture — concentrate brewed and mixed on the spot, in a smaller form factor. That single change dropped the minimum viable deployment down to 50+ unit buildings: small apartments, banks, doctor's offices — an addressable market roughly ten times larger than before.
